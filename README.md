#  Research Publication

### A Pilot Study on RNN-Based Promoter Region Identification in DNA Sequences

**Journal:** International Journal of Advance and Innovative Research (IJAIR)  
**Volume:** 13 • **Issue:** 2 (XIII) • **Publication:** April–June 2026  

**Authors:** Ankit Bind, Chetana Tanavade, Sumitkumar Tripathi

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20354050

> This research presents a Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)-based framework for automated promoter region identification in DNA sequences. The proposed model combines embedding layers with SimpleRNN architecture and dropout regularization to effectively capture sequential nucleotide patterns. Evaluated on the UCI Promoter Gene Sequences dataset, the study demonstrates reliable classification performance and establishes a reproducible baseline for future research in computational genomics.
